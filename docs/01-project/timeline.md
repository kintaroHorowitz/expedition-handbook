# Preparation timeline

This roadmap sequences preparation for Tour des Écrins from August 2026 to the
final week of June 2027. It defines the purpose of each phase and the evidence
to review, but it is not a weekly training prescription.

!!! info "Canonical ownership"
    `data/profile.yml` is the canonical source for expedition timing and
    duration. The [objective](objective.md) owns expedition success and
    readiness principles. This page owns preparation sequencing and phase
    purpose. Detailed adaptation thresholds belong to CDB-15.

## How to use the timeline

The periods below are indicative planning windows, not hard calendar gates.
Calendar dates do not force progression. Progression depends on demonstrated
tolerance, recovery and readiness, and phases may overlap, extend or reduce
when the available evidence requires it.

No running, distance, elevation gain, pace, strength, symptom or recovery
threshold is defined here. Those details require approved adaptation rules and
detailed training plans.

## Rehabilitation

- **Indicative period:** August to September 2026.
- **Purpose:** Establish a sustainable starting point while the Achilles tendon
  remains the initial training limitation.
- **Primary emphasis:** Tolerated rehabilitation activity, walking, indoor
  cycling, strength and mobility, with any return to running kept progressive.
- **Evidence reviewed before progression:** Recent tolerance, recovery,
  consistency and whether activity can continue without a worsening pattern.
- **Conditions for adapting the phase:** Extend, reduce or overlap the phase
  when tolerance or recovery does not support progression, or when current
  professional guidance requires a change.
- **Future detailed training page:** `training/rehabilitation.md`.

## Base

- **Indicative period:** October to December 2026.
- **Purpose:** Develop consistent general preparation before increasing
  expedition-specific demands.
- **Primary emphasis:** Sustainable aerobic activity, walking, strength and
  mobility, while continuing the progressive return from the initial
  limitation.
- **Evidence reviewed before progression:** Consistency across the phase,
  tolerance of the current mix of activities, recovery between sessions and
  readiness for a gradual increase in demand.
- **Conditions for adapting the phase:** Retain rehabilitation priorities,
  extend base work or reduce demands when consistency, tolerance or recovery is
  not yet sufficient for the build phase.
- **Future detailed training page:** `training/base.md`.

## Reduced-load Christmas period

- **Indicative period:** Approximately one week around Christmas and New Year,
  with exact dates to be set later.
- **Purpose:** Provide a deliberate reduction in load while preserving
  continuity through the holiday period.
- **Primary emphasis:** Recovery, flexible low-demand activity and maintaining
  established routines without trying to advance the plan.
- **Evidence reviewed before progression:** Recovery from the preceding base
  work and readiness to resume normal preparation after the reduced-load week.
- **Conditions for adapting the phase:** Extend the reduction when recovery or
  circumstances require it; resume gradually rather than compensating for
  missed work.
- **Future detailed training page:** `training/base.md`.

## Build

- **Indicative period:** January to February 2027.
- **Purpose:** Progress from general consistency toward greater hiking and
  repeat-day capacity.
- **Primary emphasis:** Gradually increasing hiking demands, climbing and
  descending exposure, strength, and the ability to recover for subsequent
  activity.
- **Evidence reviewed before progression:** Tolerance of increased demand,
  recovery across repeated activity and the absence of a deteriorating pattern
  that would undermine mountain-specific work.
- **Conditions for adapting the phase:** Return emphasis to base or
  rehabilitation work, reduce demand or extend the build when current evidence
  does not support progression.
- **Future detailed training page:** `training/build.md`.

## Mountain-specific

- **Indicative period:** March to April 2027.
- **Purpose:** Make preparation more representative of consecutive mountain
  days and the conditions expected during the expedition.
- **Primary emphasis:** Sustained ascent and descent, technical terrain,
  consecutive hiking days, and progressive use of expedition equipment and the
  approved pack approach.
- **Evidence reviewed before progression:** Durability across representative
  activity, recovery for the next day, equipment tolerance and readiness for a
  multi-day simulation.
- **Conditions for adapting the phase:** Reduce specificity, extend the phase
  or revisit build priorities when terrain, equipment, tolerance or recovery
  evidence is insufficient.
- **Future detailed training page:** `training/mountain-specific.md`.

## Simulation

- **Indicative period:** May to early June 2027.
- **Purpose:** Test preparation in representative multi-day conditions before
  the final readiness decision.
- **Primary emphasis:** Consecutive hiking days with expedition equipment,
  recovery between days, pack use, fuelling, hydration and practical routines.
- **Evidence reviewed before progression:** The representative multi-day block,
  day-to-day durability, recovery, equipment performance and unresolved issues
  relevant to the six-day objective.
- **Conditions for adapting the phase:** Modify the simulation, revisit an
  earlier priority or reconsider the expedition approach when the evidence does
  not support responsible continuation.
- **Future detailed training page:** `training/simulation.md`.

## Taper and departure

- **Indicative period:** The final weeks of June 2027 through departure in the
  final week of the month.
- **Purpose:** Reduce accumulated fatigue while retaining readiness and making
  the final expedition decisions from current evidence.
- **Primary emphasis:** Recovery, maintaining established capability, resolving
  practical issues, and avoiding unnecessary new training demands.
- **Evidence reviewed before progression:** Simulation outcomes, current
  recovery, the formal six-day readiness decision about two weeks before
  departure, and the final safety and logistics re-check within 48 hours of
  departure.
- **Conditions for adapting the phase:** Increase recovery, change the
  expedition duration or adjust departure plans when readiness, safety,
  conditions or logistics require it.
- **Future detailed training page:** `training/taper.md`.

## Monthly checkpoints

Each checkpoint is a review theme, not a numerical pass/fail threshold.

- **August 2026:** Establish the tolerated rehabilitation baseline and identify
  which activities can be performed consistently.
- **September 2026:** Review whether tolerance and recovery support a transition
  toward base preparation.
- **October 2026:** Confirm that general aerobic, walking, strength and mobility
  work can be maintained consistently.
- **November 2026:** Review continuity, recovery and tolerance of gradual
  progression within the base phase.
- **December 2026:** Assess the base period and plan the reduced-load Christmas
  week without attempting to compensate before or after it.
- **January 2027:** Confirm readiness to resume normal preparation and begin the
  build phase after the reduced-load period.
- **February 2027:** Review tolerance of increased hiking and repeat-day demands
  before greater mountain specificity.
- **March 2027:** Assess the transition to more representative ascent, descent,
  terrain and equipment use.
- **April 2027:** Review consecutive-day durability, recovery and practical
  readiness for simulation.
- **May 2027:** Evaluate representative multi-day work and identify unresolved
  equipment, recovery or routine issues.
- **June 2027:** Review the simulation evidence, make the formal six-day
  readiness decision, taper, and complete the final safety and logistics
  re-check.
