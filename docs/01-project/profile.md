# Profile

This page describes the current planning profile and the historical experience
used to calibrate preparation for Tour des Écrins. Historical achievements show
what was demonstrated at the time; they are not evidence of present fitness.

!!! info "Canonical profile data"
    `data/profile.yml` is the canonical structured source for height, current
    and historical body weight, and historical backpack weight. Values repeated
    here are included only to make the narrative understandable.

## Current expedition profile

The current structured profile records a height of 190 cm and an approximate
body-weight snapshot of 124 kg. That snapshot has no observation date, so it
must not be treated as a precise or continuously current measurement. When it
changes, `data/profile.yml` should be updated instead of maintaining a separate
value on this page.

Present readiness must be assessed using current, expedition-relevant evidence.
The historical background below provides context for planning, but does not
establish current endurance, durability or technical capacity.

## Historical background

The product owner has substantial historical exposure to endurance and
load-bearing activity:

- Hiking and trail experience from Réunion Island established familiarity with
  technical hiking terrain and descents.
- Nine years in the French Army included long hikes and load carriage.
- A marathon was completed in 3:14 in 2013 at a body weight of 95 kg.
- Half-Ironman Gérardmer was completed in 6:10 in 2014.
- The product owner describes descent as a particular strength.

These are historical capability indicators only and must not be extrapolated
into the present fitness profile.

## Why the Tour du Mont Blanc is the baseline

The completed seven-day Tour du Mont Blanc itinerary is the most relevant
documented multi-day reference for sustained distance, elevation change,
technical terrain and load carriage. Its Garmin records provide a measured
historical workload against which later expedition preparation can be
discussed.

It is a calibration point, not a template or proof of current readiness. Tour
des Écrins has its own objective, equipment target and readiness decision, as
defined on the [objective page](objective.md).

## Tour du Mont Blanc historical reference

The itinerary was completed in seven stages from 21 to 27 June 2025. The
historical record is:

- **Distance walked:** 175.82 km recorded by a Garmin Forerunner 955.
- **Elevation gain:** 10,384 m D+.
- **Elevation loss:** 10,439 m D-.
- **Elapsed stage time:** 52:07:06.
- **Moving time:** 39:52:09.
- **Body weight:** 113 kg.
- **Starting backpack weight:** 15 kg.
- **Hiking style:** without poles.

The distance is the actual distance recorded while walking. It is not an
official Tour du Mont Blanc route distance and does not assert exact alignment
with a standardized route.

### Route interpretation

There was no intentional route variant. Two minor incidental deviations affect
how the recorded distance should be understood:

- A blocked road forced additional walking.
- After reaching a col on one occasion, the product owner descended for about
  15 minutes to rejoin a friend, then climbed back with him for about 20
  minutes.

The friend accompanied the first three days before stopping the trek. These
events are context for the Garmin total, not separate route variants. Their
additional distance cannot be reconstructed reliably and is not estimated here.

### Pack context

The 15 kg starting pack included cooking equipment and lunch food for seven
days, including items such as pasta, rice and canned meat. Gîtes provided
breakfasts and dinners. The value is therefore a historical load-carriage
baseline; it does not establish that any specific item was unnecessary.

## Strengths demonstrated during the TMB

At the time of the TMB, completing seven consecutive stages demonstrated
multi-day endurance, sustained climbing and descending, and the ability to
carry the recorded starting load. Completing the itinerary without poles was
consistent with the product owner's normal hiking style. Technical terrain and
descent drew on established experience, with descent described by the product
owner as a strength.

These observations apply to the 2025 expedition and do not establish the same
capacity today.

## Lessons and areas for improvement

The baseline supports a small number of planning conclusions without inventing
details that were not recorded:

- The recorded distance, elevation and time show the scale of a completed
  seven-day workload that can inform later comparisons.
- The 15 kg pack is a meaningful historical load reference. Tour des Écrins
  preparation should demonstrate the separately approved maximum starting
  total backpack weight of 8 kg rather than reproduce that historical load.
- Current multi-day capacity must be demonstrated again before departure; it
  cannot be assumed from the 2025 completion or earlier endurance results.
- Minor incidental deviations should remain visible when recorded GPS totals
  are compared with planned routes.

Detailed training, fuelling, equipment and readiness rules belong in their
respective handbook sections, not in this baseline.

## Relationship to future expeditions

The TMB remains a historical comparison for later expeditions, including Tour
des Écrins and GR20, but each expedition must be evaluated against its own
route, conditions, objectives and current evidence. Completed expeditions can
add new reference points without replacing or rewriting the TMB record.

For canonical ownership, `data/profile.yml` owns reusable structured profile
and weight values, this page owns their human-readable historical context, and
the [objective page](objective.md) owns the Tour des Écrins objective and
success criteria.
